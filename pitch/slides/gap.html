Daju can use a language model to tidy what it reads and to translate replies. No model ever decides a stamp.

The honest answer to "why not ChatGPT": use it to read the letter if you like. It cannot tell you whose number that is, because it does not hold the registers pinned to a date. Daju does, and shows the row.
